FAQ

Good questions

Still stuck? Email [BOTTLE DRIVE EMAIL].

What can I donate?

Cans, bottles, juice boxes and cartons with an Alberta deposit. [CONFIRM LIST]

Do I need to be home?

Nope. Leave your bags where you told us, marked “Homegrown”, by [TIME] on Thursday.

What if I miss a Thursday?

No problem. We'll try again the next week, or you can skip a week in My pickup.

Which areas do you cover?

Calgary (starting with south, southeast, southwest and downtown) and Red Deer. [CONFIRM ZONES]

Do I get a tax receipt?

No. Homegrown is a non-profit collective, not a registered charity.

How do I change or cancel?

Open My pickup with your code and email to edit details, skip a week or stop pickups.
